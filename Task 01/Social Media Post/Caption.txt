Every cracked sidewalk in this city is waiting for an excuse to bloom.

We spent months walking past this empty curb on 4th Street, assuming someone else would fix it. Yesterday, Maya put a single native sapling into the concrete. By afternoon, three neighbors had stopped by with watering cans and potting soil.

One small action changes the place we call home. Stop waiting. Plant yours this weekend.
